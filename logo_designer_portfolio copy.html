The Mind
Behind The Mark

Philosophy

Who I Am

I'm fascinated by the relationship between how people think, what they feel, and what they see. With a background in psychology and a deep interest in visual design, I explore how colours, shapes and symbols shape perception.

The Approach

"I enjoy finding meaning in the simplest of forms, experimenting with visual symbolism and turning ideas into identities. For me, a logo isn't just something people look at — it's something they should be able to recognise, remember and connect with."

Your mind sees more than your eyes.

Illusion

Your brain connects patterns, fills gaps and finds meaning. That's what makes visual identity so powerful.

Perception

How people interpret shapes, patterns and visual information at a subconscious level.

Emotion

How colours, forms and visual associations trigger emotional responses that shape brand perception.

Memory

How simplicity and symbolism make a visual identity recognisable, sticky and enduring over time.
